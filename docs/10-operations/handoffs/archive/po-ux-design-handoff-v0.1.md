## Current Fixed Decisions
- Asymmetric Bridge: ONLY `Spotify Import` and `Apple Music Export`.
- Emotional Assetization: The focus is on making the user feel they are creating a valuable 'Asset' (Card-style detail page with cover, diary, tags).
- No In-app Player: Use "Open in External App" approach.
- Web-viewable Share Link: Public PA detail page should be beautiful and accessible without login.

## Do Not Change
- Do not add "Apple Music Import" or "Spotify Export" to the core flow.
- Do not design a complex in-app music player.
- Do not include B2B or gifting flows in this version.

## Done When
- A clear step-by-step user flow is defined.
- UI components for 'Asset Card' are specified.
- 'Import' progress and 'Export' success/failure states are covered.
- Shareable web view design is outlined.
